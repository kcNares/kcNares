I'm a **Full-Stack Developer and Software Engineer** focused on building practical, production-oriented digital products.

I enjoy working across the entire product lifecycle — from understanding the business problem and designing workflows to implementing APIs, authentication, databases, frontend experiences, background jobs, and production infrastructure.

# 🧠 Engineering Approach

I try to approach software development from both the **engineering** and **product** perspective.

```text
Understand the Problem
        ↓
Understand the Business Workflow
        ↓
Design the System
        ↓
Build the Core Logic
        ↓
Secure the Application
        ↓
Handle Edge Cases
        ↓
Test Real Scenarios
        ↓
Deploy & Monitor
        ↓
Improve
```

### Principles I Try to Follow

* **Keep business logic out of the UI**
* **Prefer clear workflows over unnecessary complexity**
* **Validate important rules at the backend**
* **Protect data integrity at the database level**
* **Design for failure and edge cases**
* **Treat authentication and authorization as first-class concerns**
